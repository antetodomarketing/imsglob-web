SAMI System

All Your Inventory, Under Control

At IMS Global we manage your consumption of MRO, PPE and industrial supplies directly at your facilities through our Vendor Managed Inventory (VMI) program. We handle the monitoring, replenishment and inventory control so your team can focus on producing, while we ensure you never run out of the materials that keep your operation running.

Benefits
  • Automatic replenishment based on real consumption
  • Reduced inventory and tied-up capital
  • Elimination of urgent purchases and critical shortages
  • Real-time visibility of consumption and stock
  • Scheduled deliveries and immediate attention
  • Guaranteed operational continuity

  • What services do you offer?
    We offer MRO supply, inventory management (VMI), safety & hygiene equipment and construction materials, with customized sourcing solutions for the industrial sector.
    How do I start working with IMS?
    Just contact us or request a quote online. Our team advises you on product selection, savings alternatives and delivery times.
    Do you work in Mexico and the United States?
    Yes. Since 2015 we operate in Mexico and the United States, adapting to the fiscal and operational requirements of each client.
    What does your supply process include?
    We equip, supply and solve the needs of the industrial sector: we evaluate market alternatives, optimize costs and ensure quality and delivery.
    How long does delivery take?
    Times depend on the material and availability. We speed up your purchasing process to deliver in the shortest time possible, prioritizing the continuity of your operation.
  • How does the SAMI system work?
    Through our SAMI system (System for Advanced Management of Inventory) we manage your inventory directly on site, with analytical visibility of consumption by user or area and optimized restocking.
    Do you provide real-time information?
    Yes. The SAMI system provides real-time data on the consumption and status of your inventory, so you make better purchasing decisions.
    What happens if a material runs out?
    With VMI management and the SAMI system we anticipate restocking to avoid shortages. If a material runs out, we prioritize its replenishment so your operation doesn't stop.
    Can you manage my inventory on site?
    Yes. We provide specialized personnel directly at your plant to manage on-site inventory, eliminating administrative costs and optimizing product consignment.
    Do you offer support and personalized attention?
    Yes. We stand out for outstanding service, effective communication and fast attention to each client's needs.
  • What payment methods do you accept?
    We accept bank transfer, credit card and corporate billing accounts for approved clients.
    Do you issue invoices for your services?
    Yes. Each service includes a detailed invoice in accordance with current tax requirements in Mexico.
    Do you handle imports and binational tax treatment?
    We manage imports and adapt to binational fiscal and operational requirements, supporting with the corresponding documentation.
    Can I request a cost breakdown?
    Of course. We offer a transparent cost breakdown, with no hidden administrative costs, so you know exactly what you're investing in.
    Do you offer credit terms for companies?
    Yes. We offer flexible billing and credit terms for qualifying business accounts, subject to approval.
