About IMS Global

Your Partner in Industrial Supply and Excellence

At IMS Global we are an industrial maintenance and supply company based in Tijuana, B.C. Since 2015 we have equipped, supplied and solved the needs of the industrial sector in Mexico and the United States, integrating cutting-edge sourcing, inventory management and on-site service so your operation never stops.
